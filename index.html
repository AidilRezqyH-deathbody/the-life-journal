✦

THE STORY SO FAR

Hello, I'm Aidil!

I am a student at SMKN 1 Blitar, majoring in Computer and Network Engineering. My interest in the digital world began in junior high school, when I became curious about how technology worked. This curiosity grew when I joined the Robotics extracurricular program and achieved several competition awards.

That experience encouraged me to continue learning and choose the TJKT program at SMKN 1 Blitar. Beyond technology, I also enjoy creative work, especially graphic design and video editing. I often create posters and edit videos for school assignments, social media content, and competitions.

My learning journey started with simple Scratch projects in junior high school and has continued into web development. Each step reminds me that progress takes curiosity, determination, and a willingness to keep practicing. This website is a place to document that journey, one experience at a time.

"The journey of a thousand lines of code begins with a single idea."
